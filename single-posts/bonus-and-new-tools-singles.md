## POST 32 — Internal cracking is a different problem than external cracking.
**Phase:** New Tools | **Target:** Integrity engineers | **CTA:** None

### LinkedIn Caption:

Most crack detection ILI tools are designed to find cracks initiating from the external surface — SCC, fatigue from environmental exposure, coating-related mechanisms.

Internal surface cracking is a different failure mode. It initiates from the inside — driven by corrosion fatigue, hydrogen-induced damage, or operational stress at the internal wall.

The detection approach must account for the crack origin direction. A tool optimized for external cracks may not detect internal surface features with the same sensitivity.

ACME Integrity's Internal Crack Survey (ICS) technology is specifically designed for cracks initiating from the internal pipe surface. Applicable in gas and liquid pipelines, wall thickness 4–13mm.

As pipelines are evaluated for hydrogen service, internal cracking from hydrogen damage becomes a primary concern. The inspection must be designed for the specific mechanism — not assumed to be covered by standard crack detection.

## POST 34 — MTM inspects pipelines without entering them.
**Phase:** New Tools | **Target:** Unpiggable operators | **CTA:** None

### LinkedIn Caption:

Some pipelines cannot accommodate any internal tool — no access, no traps, no bore clearance, no shutdown window.

Magnetic Tomography Method (MTM) inspects the pipeline from the outside. A walk-along survey measures distortions in the earth's magnetic field caused by stress concentration, developing corrosion, and fatigue damage in the pipe wall.

No pipeline entry. No product disruption. No shutdown required.

The survey covers up to 10 km per day. Detected anomalies are classified into three risk categories — immediate action, scheduled assessment, or monitoring. Results are GPS-referenced for precise location.

ACME Integrity MTM surveys are deployed on underground and offshore pipelines from 100mm to 1400mm diameter — providing integrity data for lines that were previously considered uninspectable.

## POST 35 — A 56-inch pipeline with no flow can still be inspected autonomously.
**Phase:** New Tools | **Target:** Large-diameter operators | **CTA:** None

### LinkedIn Caption:

Large-diameter pipelines without flow or pressure have traditionally been outside the scope of conventional ILI.

No differential pressure means no tool propulsion. No flow means no speed control. Standard ILI requires both.

ACME Integrity's self-propelled crawler technology operates independently of pipeline flow. Onboard electric motors drive the tool through the line. Onboard batteries provide up to 60 km range on a single charge. The tool moves bi-directionally, with remote start and stop capability.

Available with caliper for geometry assessment and MFL for metal loss detection. Operating temperature range: -60°C to +40°C.

Applicable for pre-commissioning inspection without the need to fill the pipeline — and for in-service pipelines where flow-driven ILI is not feasible.

The pipeline does not need to change. The inspection approach does.

## POST 36 — Hydrogen changes the integrity question.
**Phase:** New Tools | **Target:** Gas operators / energy transition | **CTA:** None

### LinkedIn Caption:

A pipeline that operated safely in natural gas service for 30 years may not be safe for hydrogen without re-assessment.

Hydrogen interacts with steel differently. Embrittlement reduces ductility. Fatigue crack growth rates increase. Sub-critical features that were stable in gas service may become active in hydrogen.

The integrity question shifts from "what is the current condition" to "is this pipeline suitable for a different service."

This requires baseline inspection data, material verification, and fitness-for-service evaluation against hydrogen-specific criteria.

ACME Integrity presented at PPIM 2026 alongside Saudi Aramco on non-destructive technologies for hydrogen pipeline assessment — including EMAT for baseline crack inspection and Internal Crack Survey (ICS) for hydrogen-induced internal damage detection.

The energy transition depends on pipeline infrastructure. Pipeline infrastructure depends on integrity data.
