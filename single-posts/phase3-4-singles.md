**Phase:** 3 | **Target:** Sour pipeline operators | **CTA:** None

### LinkedIn Caption:

Pipelines carrying H₂S-containing products face a different threat landscape.

Sour service accelerates hydrogen-induced damage — HIC, SOHIC, SSC. These mechanisms produce cracking distinct from SCC, requiring specific detection approaches.

The inspection technology must be compatible with the product. The integrity assessment must account for hydrogen-related damage.

The product defines the threat. The threat defines the inspection.

## POST 24 — Black powder in gas pipelines is not just a flow problem.
**Phase:** 4 | **Target:** Gas operators | **CTA:** None

### LinkedIn Caption:

Black powder — iron sulfide and iron oxide particles — accumulates in gas pipelines over time.

It reduces flow efficiency. It contaminates downstream equipment. And it obstructs ILI sensor surfaces during inspection.

In gas lines with significant accumulation, pre-inspection cleaning is a prerequisite for reliable ILI data.

ACME Integrity cleaning campaigns include progressive pigging with support wheels for sustained effectiveness, and cyclone dust separation down to 5 microns — without interrupting operations.

Clean sensors produce clean data. The cleaning campaign is part of the inspection program.
