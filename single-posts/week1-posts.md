## CAROUSEL 5: "Standard MFL Has a Blind Spot"
**File:** carousels/05-why-mfl-misses-longitudinal-defects.html

### LinkedIn Caption:
```
Standard MFL has been the backbone of pipeline inspection since 1969.

It's reliable. It's proven.
And it has a blind spot.

MFL applies a magnetic field along the pipe axis.
Circumferential defects disrupt the field — detected.
Longitudinal defects align with the field — missed.

Not because the tool failed.
Because of physics.

Axial grooving. Tunnel corrosion.
Longitudinal seam weld anomalies.
These can sit undetected in a standard MFL report.

That's why Transverse Field Inspection (TFI) exists.
Same principle. Different field direction.
Catches what standard MFL can't.

The question for your next planning cycle:
Is your field orientation covering your threat orientation?
```
