IN PRACTICE

Metal loss reduces wall thickness. Cracking reduces structural continuity.

Both threaten the pipeline. Neither can be assessed using the other's methodology.

Different failure mechanisms. Different assessment standards. Different inspection technologies.

ACME Integrity
THE DIFFERENCE

How each threatens the pipeline — and how each is assessed.

Metal loss

Assessed by remaining wall, feature dimensions, operating pressure. Growth is typically gradual.

Cracking

Assessed by crack depth, length, orientation, material toughness. Can propagate rapidly once critical size is reached.

THE INSIGHT

A pipeline with no reportable metal loss can still have critical cracking.

These threats coexist — but they are invisible to each other's inspection technology.

Addressing one does not address the other.

ACME Integrity INSPECTION APPROACH

Metal loss and cracking addressed as separate integrity questions — or combined in a single run.

Metal loss — MFL and UTWM. Tri-Axial MFL available for complex feature characterization.

Cracking — UTCD in liquid lines. EMAT in gas lines. ICS for internal surface cracks.

Combined — combo runs delivering both answers in one mobilization.

Inspection strategy designed around the full threat profile — not a single technology.
