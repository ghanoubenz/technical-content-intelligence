IN PRACTICE

Conventional ILI tools are propelled by product flow.

When there is no flow — or not enough pressure to drive the tool — conventional ILI is not feasible. But the integrity question remains.

The pipeline still needs integrity data. The delivery method must adapt.

CRAWLER TECHNOLOGY

A crawler moves independently of pipeline flow.

Motorized propulsion

High-torque wheels with independent suspension. Traction maintained on any pipe surface.

Tethered or autonomous

Tethered crawlers for shorter runs with external power. Autonomous crawlers with onboard batteries for long-range inspection.

Same measurement physics

UTWM, MFL, or Caliper sensors integrated into the crawler platform.

THE INSIGHT

The measurement technology does not change. Only the propulsion method changes.

Wall thickness, metal loss, geometry — the data quality from a crawler-based inspection is equivalent to a flow-driven tool operating within specification.

Different delivery. Same integrity answer.
