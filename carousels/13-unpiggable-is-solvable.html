THE APPROACHES

Different constraints require different access and propulsion methods.

Self-propelled crawlers

Motorized tools that move independently of flow. Tethered or autonomous with onboard batteries.

Single-entry access

Tool launched and retrieved from the same point. Bi-directional inspection. Temporary traps.

Non-contact external survey

MTM — detects stress concentration and developing damage without entering the pipeline.
