THE REALITY

What grows between inspections:

Internal corrosion — 0.1 to 0.5 mm/year (faster in wet gas/CO2)
SCC (Stress Corrosion Cracking) — silent until it ruptures
Fatigue cracks — from pressure cycling at seam welds
External corrosion — under failed coating + CP shielding
Dent + metal loss — interaction becomes critical over time

THE INSIGHT

The real risk isn't the defect you found last time.

It's how much it has grown since.

A minor feature at 20% wall loss can reach 50%+ in 5 years.

A small crack can propagate to critical length in fewer pressure cycles than you'd expect.
