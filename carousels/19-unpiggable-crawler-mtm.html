IN PRACTICE

"Unpiggable" typically means no flow, no pressure, no traps, or restricted access.

These are operational constraints. Each one has an engineering solution — if the inspection technology is designed for it.

The integrity question does not change because the pipeline is difficult to access.

ACME Integrity
INSPECTION WITHOUT FLOW

Different constraints require different propulsion methods.

Crawler technology

Motorized, self-propelled tools that move independently of product flow. Tethered or autonomous.

Bi-directional tools

Single-entry access. Inspect forward and return from the same point.

Non-contact methods

External survey technologies that assess pipeline condition without entering the line.

THE INSIGHT

The inspection question remains the same. Only the delivery method changes.

Metal loss, cracking, geometry — the integrity data requirements do not change because the pipeline is difficult to access.

The measurement physics are the same. The engineering challenge is getting the tool there.

ACME Integrity — UNPIGGABLE SOLUTIONS

ACME Integrity has developed multiple self-propelled and non-contact technologies for pipelines without conventional pigging capability.

Crawler + UTWM — motorized tethered system for low-flow liquid lines. Wall measurement up to 34mm. Navigates 72% bore restrictions.

Self-propelled caliper + MFL — autonomous crawler with onboard batteries. Up to 60 km range. No flow or pressure required. 56".

MTM — non-contact external survey. Detects stress concentration and developing corrosion without pipeline entry. Up to 10 km/day.

Each solution is purpose-engineered for the specific pipeline — not a modified standard tool.
