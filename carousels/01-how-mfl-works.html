THE PROBLEM

MFL is the most used ILI technology in the world.

But most people can't explain what actually happens inside the pipe.

That's a problem.

Because if you don't understand how MFL works, you can't understand what it misses.

ACME Integrity
THE REALITY

Here's what happens inside the pipe:

1

The tool carries powerful magnets that saturate the pipe wall with a magnetic field.

2

Where the wall is intact, the field flows smoothly through the steel.

3

Where there's metal loss, the field "leaks" outward.

4

Sensors on the tool measure that leakage. That's MFL.

THE INSIGHT

What MFL Finds:

✓Internal corrosion
✓External corrosion
✓Gouges and dents with metal loss
✓General wall thinning

What MFL Does NOT Find:

✗Cracks (use UTCD)
✗Coating condition (use EMAT)
✗Precise wall thickness (use UTWM)

THE TOOL CONNECTION

No single tool does everything.
That's why tool selection matters.

Cracks? → UTCD (Ultrasonic Crack Detection)
Coating? → EMAT (Electromagnetic Acoustic)
Wall thickness? → UTWM (Ultrasonic Wall Measurement)
Metal loss? → MFL (Magnetic Flux Leakage)
