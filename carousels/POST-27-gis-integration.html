IN PRACTICE

ILI data tells the operator what is inside the pipeline. GIS tells the operator where the pipeline is.

Combined, every feature has a location — not just a distance from the launcher, but a coordinate on a map.

The same feature at 40% wall loss carries different weight depending on where it is.

THE VALUE

Location changes the consequence assessment.

Near a river crossing

Environmental consequence is high. The same feature in open terrain carries lower risk.

Under a road or railway

Access for repair is restricted. Planning requires precise location data.

Adjacent to populated areas

Safety consequence is highest. Features in high-consequence areas drive repair priority.

THE INSIGHT

A feature report without geographic context is incomplete.

"Feature at KP 42.3" tells the analyst what to look at. "Feature at KP 42.3, river crossing, populated area 200m" tells the analyst how urgently to respond.

Location-aware integrity decisions are better integrity decisions.
