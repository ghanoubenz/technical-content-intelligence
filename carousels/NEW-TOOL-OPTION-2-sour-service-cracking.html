THE MECHANISM

Hydrogen enters
the steel.

In sour service, H₂S dissociates at the pipe wall. Atomic hydrogen diffuses into the steel lattice and accumulates at inclusions and grain boundaries.

The result: internal blisters, laminations, and through-wall cracks that never show on the external surface.

02 / 06

THREE THREATS

Three forms of
sour service damage.

HIC — Hydrogen-Induced Cracking

Internal laminations running parallel to the pipe wall. Invisible from outside.

SOHIC — Stress-Oriented HIC

Stacked laminations that connect through the wall thickness under stress.

SSC — Sulfide Stress Cracking

Sharp, planar cracks in high-hardness regions — HAZ, welds, cold-worked areas.

THE INSIGHT

MFL alone
is not enough

Magnetic flux leakage is designed to detect volumetric metal loss. It is not optimized for planar cracks or mid-wall laminations.

If your pipeline carries sour product, the question isn't "are we running ILI?" It's "are we running the ILI that actually finds HIC?"
