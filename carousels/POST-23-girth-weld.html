IN PRACTICE

Nearly every pipeline has girth weld anomalies.

Most are manufacturing artifacts — porosity, minor misalignment, root concavity. The question is not whether anomalies exist.

It is whether they are growing, interacting, or located in high-consequence areas.

THE DISTINCTION

Manufacturing artifact vs. active threat.

Static artifacts

Porosity, minor misalignment, root features — present since construction, stable, non-growing.

Active threats

Fatigue cracks, lack-of-fusion propagating under cyclic loading, corrosion at the weld toe.

Location matters

The same anomaly at a road crossing vs. remote location carries different consequence weight.

THE INSIGHT

Not every girth weld anomaly is a problem. Knowing the difference requires the right data.

Crack-specific detection differentiates between volumetric manufacturing artifacts and planar crack-like features — the ones that grow.

The right data separates the features that can be deferred from those that cannot.
