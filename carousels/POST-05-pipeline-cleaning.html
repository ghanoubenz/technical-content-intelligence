Debris, wax, and black powder directly impact ILI data quality.

THE IMPACT

What cleaning removes determines what the inspection can find.

Wax and paraffin

Coats sensor surfaces, reduces ultrasonic coupling and magnetic contact.

Black powder

Iron sulfide/oxide particles obstruct MFL sensors in gas lines.

Scale and debris

Creates signal noise that masks real features.
