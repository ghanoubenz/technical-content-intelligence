THE TECHNOLOGY

MTM measures magnetic field distortions from the surface.

Walk-along survey

Equipment carried along the pipeline route — above ground or seabed. Up to 10 km per day.

Magnetic field analysis

Stress concentration, developing corrosion, and fatigue damage distort the earth's magnetic field at the pipe wall.

3-tier risk classification

Anomalies classified: immediate action, scheduled assessment, or monitoring. GPS-referenced.
