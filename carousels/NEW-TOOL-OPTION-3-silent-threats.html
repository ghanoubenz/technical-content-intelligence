THE PROBLEM

Stress corrosion cracking
develops in silence.

Under disbonded coating, moisture reaches bare steel. With the right chemistry and tensile stress, fine cracks begin to grow — branched, tight, and nearly invisible.

No external bulge. No pressure drop. No warning. Until rupture.

WHY IT'S MISSED

MFL was built for
metal loss — not planar cracks.

Geometry mismatch

A crack removes almost no volume. The flux leakage signal is weak or absent.

Orientation sensitivity

Axial MFL is least sensitive to the exact crack direction SCC prefers.

Signal ambiguity

When MFL sees a crack at all, the signature is hard to distinguish from benign features.
