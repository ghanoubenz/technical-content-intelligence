IN PRACTICE

Black powder accumulates in gas pipelines over time.

Iron sulfide and iron oxide particles. It reduces flow efficiency, contaminates downstream equipment, and obstructs ILI sensor surfaces during inspection.

Clean sensors produce clean data. The cleaning campaign is part of the inspection program.

THE IMPACT

Black powder affects more than pipeline flow.

Sensor obstruction

MFL sensors coated with magnetic particles produce noisy, unreliable data.

Reduced detection

Features masked by deposits may be undersized or missed entirely.

Equipment contamination

Downstream valves, compressors, and metering equipment affected by particulate carry-over.

THE INSIGHT

Pre-inspection cleaning is a prerequisite for reliable ILI data in gas lines with significant accumulation.

The cleaning campaign is not separate from the inspection program. It is the first phase of it.

The data quality of the inspection is determined before the tool enters the line.

ACME Integrity CLEANING SOLUTIONS

ACME Integrity cleaning campaigns remove black powder without interrupting operations.

Progressive pigging — staged cleaning with support wheels for sustained effectiveness.

Cyclone dust separation — particles down to 5 microns removed at the receiver. No operational interruption.

Tailored programs — cleaning intensity matched to pipeline condition and deposit severity.
