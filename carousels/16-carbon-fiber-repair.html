IN PRACTICE

When ILI identifies metal loss requiring reinforcement, the default response is often steel sleeve or cut-out replacement.

Both require significant planning, welding, and — in many cases — pipeline shutdown.

There is an alternative that keeps the pipeline in operation.

THE APPROACH

Carbon fiber composite repair restores structural strength without welding or shutdown.

The material wraps around the affected area. It is installed while the pipeline remains in operation. It is engineered to restore the pipe to its design strength at the corroded section.

No hot work. No shutdown. No pipe replacement.

THE INSIGHT

The cost of a repair includes more than materials and labor.

Shutdown time. Production loss. Mobilization. Environmental risk of cut-out operations. Composite repair reduces the total cost of integrity management.

The fastest repair is the one that does not require stopping the pipeline.
