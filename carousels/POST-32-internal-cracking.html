IN PRACTICE

Most crack detection ILI tools are designed for external surface cracks.

SCC, fatigue from environmental exposure, coating-related mechanisms — all initiate from the outside. Internal surface cracking is a different failure mode.

A tool optimized for external cracks may not detect internal features with the same sensitivity.

INTERNAL CRACK MECHANISMS

What causes cracks from the inside.

Corrosion fatigue

Cyclic loading combined with corrosive product initiates cracking from the internal wall.

Hydrogen-induced damage

H₂ diffusion into the steel causes internal blistering and cracking — HIC, SOHIC.

Operational stress

Pressure cycling and flow-induced vibration create stress concentrations at the internal surface.

THE INSIGHT

As pipelines are evaluated for hydrogen service, internal cracking becomes a primary concern.

The inspection must be designed for the specific mechanism — not assumed to be covered by standard external crack detection.

Different origin. Different propagation. Different detection requirement.
