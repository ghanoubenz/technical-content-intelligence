Coating disbondment is not a defect.

It is a future defect location.

IN PRACTICE

Coating protects the pipe — until it separates from it.

Once disbonded, the steel is exposed to soil moisture, CO₂, and near-neutral pH conditions.

CP shielding begins. The protection system can no longer reach the exposed surface.

2 / 6
ACME Integrity
THE MECHANISM

Disbondment creates the conditions where SCC initiates.

Coating separates

CP current is shielded from the pipe surface.

Environment develops

Near-neutral or high-pH conditions form — both documented SCC precursors.

Cracking initiates

Under sustained stress, micro-cracks form at the exposed surface.

ACME Integrity
THE INSIGHT

Disbondment identified today may have no active cracking yet.

But it marks where cracking is most likely to develop.

This is not current-condition data. It is forward-looking integrity information.

HOW THIS IS ADDRESSED

ACME Integrity EMAT inspections detect cracks and coating condition in a single run.

Cracks and SCC — detected at 0.2t depth, POD 90%. Axial features, seam weld anomalies.

Coating disbondment — mapped at 100 mm and above, POD 90%. Prioritized for monitoring or direct assessment.

Gas and liquid pipelines — no couplant required. 120% overlapping surface coverage.

Applied in inspection programs where SCC susceptibility is part of the threat assessment.
