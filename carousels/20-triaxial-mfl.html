IN PRACTICE

Standard MFL uses axial magnetization. It measures flux leakage along one primary direction.

For general corrosion and pitting, this is effective. For complex-shaped features — a single axis may average out the true geometry.

Complex metal loss requires more than one measurement direction.

THE TECHNOLOGY

Tri-Axial MFL measures magnetic flux leakage in three independent axes.

Axial component

Detects circumferentially oriented features.

Radial component

Measures depth and volume of metal loss.

Circumferential component

Detects axially oriented features and complex shapes.

Three measurements. One feature. A more complete characterization.

THE INSIGHT

Complex metal loss features are not always simple pits or uniform wall thinning.

Selective seam corrosion, groove-like features, and irregular metal loss patterns are better characterized when measured from multiple magnetic axes simultaneously.

More measurement axes means less ambiguity in feature characterization.
