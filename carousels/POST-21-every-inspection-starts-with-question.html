THE SEQUENCE

The correct sequence: question → technology → data → assessment.

Step 1: Threat assessment

What are the active threats? What failure mechanisms are possible given the product, environment, and history?

Step 2: Technology selection

Which ILI technology — or combination — addresses those specific threats?

Step 3: Data → Decision

The data defines the assessment. The assessment drives the integrity decision.
