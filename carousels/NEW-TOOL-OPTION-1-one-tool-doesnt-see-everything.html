THE PHYSICS

Metal loss and cracking
aren't the same threat.

A corrosion pit removes material in volume. A crack separates material along a plane.

Different geometry. Different physics. Different detection method required.

ACME Integrity

02 / 06

TWO TECHNOLOGIES

Each built for a specific
type of defect.

MFL — Magnetic Flux Leakage

Detects volumetric metal loss. Ideal for corrosion, pitting, general wall loss.

UTCD — Ultrasonic Crack Detection

Detects planar flaws. Designed for SCC, fatigue cracks, and longitudinal seam weld anomalies.

Used together

The combination gives a complete picture of pipeline condition in a single campaign.
