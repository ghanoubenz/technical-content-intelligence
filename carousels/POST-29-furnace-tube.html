IN PRACTICE

Furnace tubes operate under extreme conditions.

High temperature, thermal cycling, coke buildup. Cracking, carburization, and wall thinning develop over time.

A tube failure during operation forces an unplanned shutdown. The cost is measured in days of lost production.

THE THREATS

What develops inside furnace tubes over time.

Thermal cracking

Repeated heating and cooling cycles create thermal fatigue cracks in the tube wall.

Carburization

Carbon absorption changes the metallurgy — reducing ductility and increasing brittleness.

Wall thinning

Oxidation and erosion from coke and process flow reduce the tube wall over time.
