IN PRACTICE

ILI tools require differential pressure to move through the line.

In low-pressure systems, there may not be enough driving force. Tool stalling becomes a real risk.

The pipeline that is hardest to inspect is often the one that needs it most.

ACME Integrity
THE CHALLENGE

Low pressure affects more than tool propulsion.

Speed instability — inconsistent velocity degrades sensor sampling.

Insufficient bypass — tool may block flow rather than move with it.

No pressurization option — pipeline cannot always be modified to accommodate inspection.

THE INSIGHT

The challenge is not the technology. It is the operating condition.

The same tool that performs reliably at 40 bar may not complete a run at 4 bar without engineering adaptation.

Difficult pipelines require customized solutions — not standard deployments.
