IN PRACTICE

An ILI report lists features — type, size, location.

It tells the operator what is there. It does not tell the operator what to do about it.

Between data and decision, there is engineering assessment.

THE INSIGHT

Two pipelines with identical ILI findings may require completely different responses.

Operating pressure, material grade, design factor, consequence of failure — all affect the outcome.

The same feature is critical in one pipeline and acceptable in another.
