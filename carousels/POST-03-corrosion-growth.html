The growth model behind the assessment determines its reliability.

THE INSIGHT

A fixed re-inspection interval does not reflect actual pipeline condition.

Some features grow faster than expected. Others remain stable. Only measurement reveals which is which.

The difference between assumed and measured growth is the difference between guessing and engineering.
