IN PRACTICE

Some pipelines operate above standard temperature limits.

Heavy crude, steam-assisted operations, near-wellhead facilities. Standard ILI tools are not rated for these conditions.

The inspection technology must be rated for the actual operating temperature.

ACME Integrity
THE EFFECTS

Elevated temperature affects every component of the ILI tool.

Sensor performance

Piezoelectric and Hall effect sensors have temperature-dependent response characteristics.

Battery capacity

High temperature reduces battery capacity and can cause thermal runaway in standard cells.

Elastomer seals

Cups and seals degrade at elevated temperatures — affecting bypass, propulsion, and wall contact.

THE INSIGHT

A tool rated for 50°C cannot be deployed at 80°C without adaptation.

The operating temperature is not an inconvenience. It is a design parameter that must be engineered into the tool configuration.

ACME Integrity TEMPERATURE RATINGS

ACME Integrity adapts tool configurations for actual operating temperatures.

MFL — up to +85°C. Elastomer compounds and battery configurations adapted per project.

UTWM — up to +75°C. EMAT up to +70°C. Sensor calibration adjusted for temperature.

Self-propelled crawlers — -60°C to +40°C. Extreme cold and moderate temperature environments.

Pre-run engineering assessment determines tool configuration for each project.
