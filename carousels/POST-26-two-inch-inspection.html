IN PRACTICE

2-inch pipelines carry product under pressure. They corrode. They degrade. They require integrity data.

But fitting sensors, electronics, batteries, and magnetization into a space smaller than a fist is an extreme engineering challenge.

Small diameter is not a reason for reduced integrity expectations.

THE CHALLENGE

What makes small-diameter ILI difficult.

Sensor density

Resolution must be maintained despite the small circumference — fewer sensors means coarser data.

Power and endurance

Battery volume is minimal. Power must last the full run despite reduced battery capacity.

Navigability

Tight bends, small bore restrictions. The tool must navigate the full pipeline geometry.

THE INSIGHT

A scaled-down tool is not a small-diameter tool.

Miniaturizing a larger tool loses performance. A purpose-built small-diameter tool is designed from the ground up for the specific constraints.

The performance specification must be met — regardless of the diameter.
