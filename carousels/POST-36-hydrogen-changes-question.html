IN PRACTICE

A pipeline safe for 30 years in natural gas may not be safe for hydrogen.

Hydrogen interacts with steel differently. Embrittlement reduces ductility. Fatigue crack growth rates increase. Sub-critical features may become active.

The integrity question shifts fundamentally when the service changes.

THE REQUIREMENTS

What hydrogen service conversion requires.

Baseline inspection data

Complete condition assessment — cracks, metal loss, seam welds, geometry — before any conversion decision.

Material verification

Steel grade, hardness, weld properties evaluated against hydrogen compatibility criteria.

Fitness-for-service evaluation

Every existing feature reassessed against hydrogen-specific failure criteria.

THE INSIGHT

What was acceptable in natural gas may not be acceptable in hydrogen.

Features that were stable and sub-critical in gas service may propagate under hydrogen conditions. The same pipeline requires a completely different assessment framework.

The conversion decision depends on inspection data collected before the conversion.
