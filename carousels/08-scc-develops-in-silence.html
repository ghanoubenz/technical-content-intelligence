THE MECHANISM

SCC requires three simultaneous conditions.

Susceptible material

Certain steel grades and microstructures.

Tensile stress

Operating pressure, residual stress, or soil loading.

Corrosive environment

Near-neutral pH or high-pH conditions at the pipe surface.

Remove any one — and SCC does not initiate.

THE INSIGHT

SCC colonies can exist for years with no operational indication.

Growth is slow — until it reaches critical depth. Then propagation accelerates.

The inspection window is before the growth curve steepens — not after.

HOW THIS IS ADDRESSED

ACME Integrity crack inspection technologies are designed for SCC-susceptible pipelines.

UTCD — shear wave ultrasonic in liquid lines. ±1mm depth sizing. SCC, HIC, fatigue, seam weld cracks.

EMAT — electromagnetic detection in gas lines. No couplant. Coating disbondment mapping included.

ICS — Internal Crack Survey for cracks initiating from the internal surface. Hydrogen-induced damage, corrosion fatigue.

Inspection strategy matched to the pipeline's SCC susceptibility profile.
