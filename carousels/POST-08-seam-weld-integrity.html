INSPECTION STRATEGY

The pipe body and the seam weld fail differently.

They must be inspected differently.

ACME Integrity
IN PRACTICE

Longitudinal seam welds can contain manufacturing anomalies that remain dormant for decades.

Lack of fusion, hook cracks, hard spots — features that become active under fatigue or pressure cycling. Inspecting the pipe body for corrosion does not address seam weld integrity.

These are separate integrity questions requiring separate technologies.

2 / 6
ACME Integrity
THE THREATS

Seam weld failure mechanisms are distinct from pipe body threats.

Manufacturing anomalies

Lack of fusion, cold welds, laminations from the original production process.

Hook cracks

Curved cracks at the fusion line that propagate under cyclic loading.

Selective seam corrosion

Preferential corrosion along the weld zone due to metallurgical differences.

ACME Integrity
THE INSIGHT

Standard axial MFL detects circumferential features. Seam weld anomalies are longitudinal.

The magnetization direction must match the feature orientation. Axial MFL is optimized for circumferential metal loss — not for longitudinal seam weld features.

The wrong magnetization direction means the feature is invisible to the sensor.

ACME Integrity SEAM WELD ASSESSMENT

ACME Integrity addresses seam weld integrity with purpose-selected technologies.

TFI (Transverse Field Inspection)

Circumferential magnetization for longitudinal feature detection in pipe body and seam weld.

EMAT

Seam weld cracking detection in gas pipelines. No couplant. Coating disbondment mapping.

Combo configurations

TFI + MFL in a single run for both pipe body and seam weld coverage.

Tool selection based on product type, threat profile, and weld history.
