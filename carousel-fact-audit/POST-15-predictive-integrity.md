## Verified Facts Used

- Predicta integrates available digital pipeline information and supports a more effective integrity program.
- Predicta uses ILI results as a base to evaluate pipeline integrity.
- Assessment outputs include ERF, safe/failure pressure, pipeline remaining life, and mitigation / repair time.
- Data integration can include elevation, CIPS, DCVG, route map, and feature characterization plots of ILI data.
- Predicta supports fitness-for-service assessment and corrosion assessment methods including B31-G, modified B31-G, DNV Part B, and Kastner.
- Corrosion growth assessment can use single inspection data plus operation data or repeated ILI inspection sets.
- Repeat inspection assessment includes data alignment, feature matching, signal review, and corrosion growth rate estimation.
- Accurate corrosion growth rate estimation is critical for repair, maintenance, and re-inspection schedules.
- Predicta supports “what if” scenario assessment and visualizes integrity status on dashboards plus 2D and 3D route maps.

## Risky Claims Avoided

- No promise that predictions become automatically more accurate over time.
- No guarantee of remaining life accuracy independent of input quality.
- No claim that Predicta replaces engineering judgment.
- No invented AI, machine learning, or autonomous decision logic.

## Visual Accuracy Check

- Use software/data visuals, pipeline route maps, integrity dashboards, timelines, and corrosion growth curves.
- Do not show fake sensors, fake tools, or physical inspection mechanisms.
- Keep visuals abstract enough that they communicate decision support without implying a specific unverified software UI.

## Tool Image Source Used

- None. Predicta is software/integrity assessment; using a physical inspection tool would risk misleading the viewer.

## Business Objective

Position ACME Integrity as a partner that helps operators convert ILI and pipeline data into defensible decisions: repair timing, re-inspection interval, remaining life, and integrity status visibility.

## Final Slide Direction

1. Hook: “ILI data should not stop at detection.”
2. Client problem: Detection alone does not decide repair timing, remaining life, or re-inspection interval.
3. Technical logic: Predicta integrates digital pipeline information and ILI results.
4. Assessment outputs: ERF, safe/failure pressure, remaining life, repair time.
5. Growth planning: Repeat ILI supports alignment, feature matching, signal review, and corrosion growth rate estimation.
6. ACME Integrity capability: dashboards, 2D/3D route maps, what-if scenarios, planning decisions.
7. CTA: turn inspection history into integrity planning.
