# 13 Unpiggable Is A Solvable Problem — Fact Gate

## Topic

Unpiggable / initially unpiggable pipelines can sometimes be made inspectable through engineered access, cleaning, tracking, and adapted inspection methods.

## Source Of Truth

- `docs/source-materials\PRESENTATIONS\ACME Integrity_Loading Lines Inspection.pptx`
- Old draft used only as concept source: `docs/source-materials\carousels\13-unpiggable-is-solvable.html`

## Verified Facts Used

- ACME Integrity documented a successful MFL inspection of initially “unpiggable” 48-inch loading lines.
- The work included an innovative solution, pig trap facility design and fabrication, adapting pigs to the pipeline configuration, cleaning, inspection, and XYZ mapping.
- The pipeline route included onshore sections crossing a highway and local road, then offshore sections to SPMs / PLEMs.
- A previous UT attempt reached around 70% of the first pipeline and indicated failure due to paraffin build-up in the bottom part of the line.
- A previous UT crawler got stuck in the onshore section of the second line and was retrieved with difficulty.
- ACME Integrity used MFL with a free-swimming aspect, pig tracking and monitoring to prevent entry into the PLEM, cleaning pigs that brought debris back onshore, and bi-directional inspection pigs.
- The pig trap arrangement was closed circuit to prevent oil exposure to the environment and minimize sludge handling.
- The project notes state that innovative design of cleaning and inspection tools solved the challenge of making the unpiggable loading lines piggable.
- Field work was completed in 22 days after design and preparation.
