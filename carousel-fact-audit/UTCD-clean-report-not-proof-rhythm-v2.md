## Verified Facts Used

- UTCD is ultrasonic crack detection technology.
- UTCD detects cracks and crack-like features.
- UTCD focuses mainly on liquid product pipelines; gas pipelines may be inspected through liquid batching.
- Smooth-wall surfaced pipes should be sufficiently clean to prevent severe echo-loss.
- UTCD tools comply with general pipeline operating conditions including pressure, temperature, flow, wall thickness, unbarred tees, and bend radii.
- UTCD can inspect for axial and circumferential cracks with configuration changes.
- UTCD detection capabilities include stress corrosion cracking, shrinkage cracks, fatigue cracks, cracks in dents, toe cracks, hook cracks, seam weld cracks, and lack of fusion.
- Internal UTCD material states circumferential resolution of 4 mm, longitudinal resolution of 1.5 mm at 1 m/s and 3.0 mm at 2 m/s.
- Internal UTCD material states crack depth sizing accuracy of 1 mm at 80% confidence level.
